# Understand the application through its technical design {#understand-the-application-through-its-technical-design}

Start with the [approach essay](/technical), then inspect the [ERD](/technical/erd), [Infrastructure](/technical/whiteboards/infrastructure) and [Data flows](/technical/whiteboards/data-flows) whiteboards. Together they explain how the application works, how its records relate, where its resources run and how information moves.

The ERD uses crow's-foot associations with explicit optionality. Named boards share diagram grammar, retain independent revision histories and can pin exact data-entity or ERD definitions. Infrastructure and flows are explanations of the actual application, not a catalog of source folders.

Older Component/System definitions and inventory stories remain available for existing pins and history. They are not required navigation or a substitute for the essay and three boards. Exact source pins and authored deprecation annotations help readers distinguish current guidance from historical context.
