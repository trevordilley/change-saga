# Explain the product before its implementation {#explain-the-product-before-its-implementation}

Product knowledge gives a reader the purpose of the application and the outcomes each feature enables. Personas are the people who benefit. Features are durable product areas; stories express their needs, and acceptance criteria describe independent observable obligations.

Feature and story essays provide rationale and boundaries. Short criterion titles help navigation, while the full statement and optional Markdown explanation stay readable. Prototypes, vocabulary, citations and design references add context without silently turning an inference into accepted intent.

Definition maturity, lifecycle and evidence availability are separate. Preserve proposals and conflicts honestly, and reassess evidence when authored obligations change. [Technical design](/technical) explains how these records connect to implementation.
