## Intended outcome {#intended-outcome}

Let the product owner map dependencies between Roadmap features and communicate prerequisites to the team alongside planned capabilities.

## First use case {#first-use-case}

[Self-hosted onboarding](urn:change-saga:change-saga:feature:self-hosted-onboarding) depends on [Self-hostable server mode](urn:change-saga:change-saga:feature:self-hostable-server). A reader should be able to understand the direction of that prerequisite relationship.

## Possible visual direction {#possible-visual-direction}

The Roadmap view may eventually present dependencies visually with an actual Gantt chart. This is an idea to develop, not a commitment to dates, durations, automatic scheduling, or a specific interaction design. Capture the features and their intent now; work out this presentation later.

## Open decisions {#open-decisions}

- The initial authoring and reading experience for dependencies.
- Whether prerequisites are advisory or constrain changes to built status.
- How relationships appear after a prerequisite becomes built.
- Circular dependency behavior and any eventual scheduling model.

## Design and handoff {#design-and-handoff}

This feature remains planned. Its proposed story captures the basic prerequisite relationship; the visual design and additional acceptance criteria remain open. Reuse the existing feature identity and planning model where appropriate.
