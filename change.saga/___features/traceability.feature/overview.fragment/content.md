# Follow intent to evidence, and evidence back to intent {#follow-intent-to-evidence-and-evidence-back-to-intent}

Traceability connects nearby facts: a story serves a persona, a design addresses a story or criterion, a test verifies an obligation, and an implementation Item explains exact code. Readers can follow these links in either direction to understand why a change matters.

Longer paths are derived from declared relationships. A source edit can reveal affected intent without requiring every record to repeat a direct link to every other record. Explicit criterion pins protect against treating old evidence as proof of a changed obligation.

Missing relationships and stale endpoints remain visible. An inferred path helps discovery; it does not authorize acceptance or establish that an explanation is correct.
